The Art of Authentic Kenyan Mbuzi Choma: Mastering Flame-Grilled Perfection & Secret Herb Rubs

In Kenyan culinary culture, Mbuzi Choma is far more than a meal—it is a cherished tradition of celebration, fellowship, and togetherness. Whether gathering family at our Kikuyu branch or hosting executive meetings at Afi Boardroom, the sizzling aroma of tender goat meat roasting over open embers signals the start of unforgettable moments.

1. The Golden Rule: Meat Selection & Pasture Origin

Great Mbuzi Choma starts long before the grill is ignited. At Afi Steaks & Platters, we source strictly free-range highland goats aged between 8 to 14 months. Goats raised on natural shrubs and acacia leaves possess lean, muscular fibers enriched with natural flavor depth. We never use frozen cuts—every leg, shoulder, and rib rack is freshly prepared daily at our in-house butchery.

2. Wood Fire Dynamics: Acacia & Fruitwood Embers

The charcoal fuel dictates 50% of the final flavor profile. Standard commercial briquettes burn cold and emit harsh chemical odours. We burn high-density natural Acacia hardwood combined with seasoned fruitwood. Acacia yields steady 450°C radiant heat required to sear the outer crust, while fruitwood releases aromatic smoke that gently infuses the meat.

“The secret to legendary Mbuzi Choma is patience. Never rush high heat over raw meat; let the slow radiant embers render the internal fat into succulent juices.”

3. Layered Seasoning: The Afi Signature Herb Rub

While purists advocate for coarse sea salt alone, our master pitmasters apply a two-stage seasoning process. First, a light massage of cold-pressed garlic oil and sea salt locks in initial moisture. In the final 10 minutes over low embers, we dust a proprietary rub of crushed rosemary, wild thyme, and toasted coriander seeds to create our signature golden crust.

4. Perfect Pairings: Fresh Kachumbari & Ugali

No Mbuzi Choma feast is complete without chilled fresh Kachumbari—diced vine tomatoes, red onions, fresh coriander, and lemon juice with a kick of bullet chilli. Accompanied by hot steaming ugali or mukimo, it delivers the ultimate Kenyan dining experience.

PORK HOUSE SPECIALTIES
By Pitmaster Afi Pork House July 28, 2026 5 Min Read

Why Honey-Glazed Pork Ribs & Crispy Pork Belly Are Taking Nairobi’s Culinary Scene by Storm

Over the past few years, Nairobi and Kiambu County have experienced an unprecedented revolution in artisanal pork cuisine. Leading this movement is Afi Pork House, where our honey-glazed pork ribs and char-grilled pork platters have earned a loyal following across Kenya.

1. The Alchemy of Wild Honey & Apple Cider Glaze

What gives Afi pork ribs their signature sticky, glossy coating? It starts with organic wild honey harvested from Rift Valley apiaries, simmered with aged apple cider vinegar, crushed ginger, star anise, and smoked paprika. Brush onto tender rib racks during the final roasting minutes, the sugars caramelize into a irresistible sweet-and-smoky glaze.

2. Low & Slow: The 4-Hour Smoke Chamber

We do not boil or microwave rib racks. Every rack undergoes a 4-hour slow smoke at 110°C in our custom-built pit chambers. This allows the collagen in the meat to melt into gelatin, ensuring the meat slides effortlessly clean off the bone at first bite.

3. Crispy Pork Crackling Secrets

For our Pork Belly and Choma cuts, achieving gold-standard crackling requires high-heat searing over open flames. By dry-curing the rind with coarse rock salt for 24 hours prior to grilling, the skin blisters into a crisp, crunchy texture that complements the tender pork meat below.
