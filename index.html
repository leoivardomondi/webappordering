The Authentic Kenyan Grill

Welcome to Afi Steaks & Platters

Home of fiery grill mastercraft, signature Mbuzi (Goat) Fry & Mbuzi Choma, honey-glazed pork, sizzling steaks, and generous group sharing boards. Known across Nairobi and Kikuyu as Afi Grill & Thrill and Afi Pork House, we bring people together with rich Kenyan flavors, vibrant atmosphere, and unbeatable hospitality.

Signature Choma & Steaks

From slow-roasted Mbuzi Choma cut to perfection to aged rib-eye steaks and honey-glazed pork belly, every cut is seasoned with our secret blend of local spices.

Live Music & Atmosphere

Dine while enjoying weekly live music sessions. Afi offers a warm, high-energy dining environment where great food meets memorable entertainment.

Private Dining & Functions
